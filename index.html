About PEC

What is PEC?

PEC provides students with a structured environment to learn programming, practice problem solving, participate in technical activities, interact with mentors and improve their skills.

Problem Solving

Develop logical and algorithmic thinking through structured challenges and practice.

Mentorship

Learn through guidance and feedback from experienced mentors in your journey.

Continuous Learning

Build consistent programming habits that lead to long-term skill development.

Collaboration

Learn and grow together with fellow students in a supportive community.

Our Activities

What We Do

PEC offers a wide range of programming activities designed to enhance your technical skills.

Weekly Coding Challenges

Regular programming problems and challenges to keep your skills sharp.

DSA Practice

Practice data structures and algorithms with curated problem sets.

Debugging Challenges

Find and fix programming bugs to strengthen your debugging skills.

Coding Competitions

Participate in coding contests and compete with fellow students.

Project Development

Build practical technical projects to apply your programming knowledge.

Mentorship Sessions

Interact with mentors and receive personalized guidance and feedback.
