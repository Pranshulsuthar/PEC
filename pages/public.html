About Us

What is PEC?

PEC provides students with a structured environment to learn programming, practice problem solving, participate in technical activities, interact with mentors and improve their skills.

Problem Solving

We encourage creative thinking and innovative approaches to problem solving.

Mentorship

We believe in helping each other grow through collaboration and mutual support.

Continuous Learning

We are committed to continuous improvement and skill development.

Collaboration

We uphold honesty, fairness, and ethical practices in everything we do.

What We Do

Weekly Coding Challenges

Regular programming problems and challenges to keep your skills sharp.

DSA Practice

Debugging Challenges

Project Development
